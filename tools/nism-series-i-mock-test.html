NISM Series I பற்றி அனைத்தும் தெரிஞ்சுக்கோ — Everything You Need to Know
📋
Exam Details
Full Name: NISM-Series-I: Currency Derivatives Certification
Conducted by: National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM)
Questions: 100 MCQ, 1 mark each
Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
Passing Marks: 60 out of 100 (60%)
Negative Marking: 25% of marks per wrong answer
Exam Fee: ~₹1,500 + GST (verify on nism.ac.in)
Certificate Validity: 3 years (renew via CPE or re-exam)
🎯
Who Needs This Certification
Mandatory for: Approved users & sales personnel dealing in the exchange-traded currency derivatives segment
Jointly Overseen by: SEBI (exchange-traded derivatives) and RBI (overall forex policy)
Also useful for: Corporate treasury professionals, forex dealers, and risk managers
Currency Pairs Covered: USD/INR, EUR/INR, GBP/INR, JPY/INR + cross-currency pairs
Step 1: Register on nism.ac.in → receive workbook
⚠️
Exam Strategy Tips
One of the Toughest NISM Exams: Nation-wide pass rate is historically below 50% — don't underestimate this one
High Weightage: Currency Futures & Options module carries ~20% of marks — study contract specs and pricing carefully
Regulatory Rules: Memorise position limits for clients/TMs/CMs — these are factual, direct-recall questions
Negative Marking: Avoid random guessing — a wrong answer costs 0.25 marks
