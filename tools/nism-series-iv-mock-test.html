NISM Series IV பற்றி அனைத்தும் தெரிஞ்சுக்கோ — Everything You Need to Know
📋
Exam Details
Full Name: NISM-Series-IV: Interest Rate Derivatives Certification
Conducted by: National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM)
Questions: 100 MCQ, 1 mark each
Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
Passing Marks: 60 out of 100 (60%)
Negative Marking: 25% of marks per wrong answer
Exam Fee: ~₹1,500 + GST (verify on nism.ac.in)
Certificate Validity: 3 years (renew via CPE or re-exam)
🎯
Who Needs This Certification
Mandatory for: Individuals dealing in or advising on Interest Rate Derivatives (IRD) products under SEBI rules
Also useful for: Treasury professionals, bond traders, and market risk managers
Eligibility: No formal minimum qualification, though basic understanding of debt markets & derivatives helps
Career Path: Bond futures/options traders, fixed-income desks, debt market intermediaries
Step 1: Register on nism.ac.in → receive workbook
⚠️
Exam Strategy Tips
Fixed-Income Foundation: Strong grasp of bond pricing, yield, and duration is essential before tackling derivatives topics
High Weightage: Futures Contracts module carries ~20% of marks — study contract specs, conversion factor & cheapest-to-deliver bond carefully
Negative Marking: Avoid random guessing — a wrong answer costs 0.25 marks
