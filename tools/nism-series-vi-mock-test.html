NISM Series VI பற்றி அனைத்தும் தெரிஞ்சுக்கோ — Everything You Need to Know
📋
Exam Details
Full Name: NISM-Series-VI: Depository Operations Certification
Conducted by: National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM)
Questions: 100 MCQ, 1 mark each
Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
Passing Marks: 60 out of 100 (60%)
Negative Marking: 25% of marks per wrong answer
Exam Fee: ~₹1,500 + GST (verify on nism.ac.in)
Certificate Validity: 3 years (renew via CPE or re-exam)
🎯
Who Needs This Certification
Mandatory for: Associated persons engaged by a registered Depository Participant (DP) in operational risk-related activities
Replaces: The earlier NSDL Depository Operations Module / BSE Central Depository Certification (pre-2011)
Mandated under: SEBI (Certification of Associated Persons in the Securities Markets) Regulations, 2007
Useful for: Back-office roles at DPs, brokers, and depositories (NSDL/CDSL) handling demat accounts
Step 1: Register on nism.ac.in → receive workbook
Step 2: Book exam slot at nearest NISM center
⚠️
Exam Strategy Tips
High Weightage: Account Opening & Account Operations chapters carry significant weightage — study these thoroughly
Negative Marking: Avoid random guessing — a wrong answer costs 0.25 marks
Process-Heavy: Many questions test operational process knowledge (forms, timelines, documentation) — memorise key procedures
