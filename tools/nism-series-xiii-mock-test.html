NISM Series XIII பற்றி அனைத்தும் தெரிஞ்சுக்கோ — Everything You Need to Know
📋
Exam Details
Full Name: NISM-Series-XIII: Common Derivatives Certification Examination
Conducted by: National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM)
Questions: 150 MCQ, 1 mark each — the LARGEST NISM derivatives exam
Duration: 3 Hours (180 Minutes)
Passing Marks: 90 out of 150 (60%)
Negative Marking: 25% of marks per wrong answer
Exam Fee: ~₹3,000 + GST (verify on nism.ac.in)
Certificate Validity: 3 years (renew via CPE or re-exam)
🎯
Who Needs This Certification
Purpose: Combines Equity, Currency & Interest Rate Derivatives into ONE certification — no need to take Series VIII, I, and IV separately
Also Used For: Eligibility to become a Specialised Investment Fund (SIF) Distributor
Eligibility: Typically requires a valid ARN or EUIN number
Best For: Multi-asset derivatives traders/advisers who need broad coverage in a single exam
Step 1: Register on nism.ac.in → PAN must be updated in registration details
⚠️
Exam Strategy Tips
Longest NISM Exam: 150 questions in 180 minutes = ~72 seconds/question average — pace yourself carefully
Not Segment-Divided: Questions from Equity/Currency/IRD are mixed throughout — you can't "skip" a weak segment
Common Concepts First: Master shared concepts (margining, MTM, SPAN) that apply across all three segments — high efficiency
Negative Marking: Avoid random guessing — a wrong answer costs 0.25 marks
