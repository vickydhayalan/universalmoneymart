NISM Series V-B பற்றி அனைத்தும் தெரிஞ்சுக்கோ — Everything You Need to Know
📋
Exam Details
Full Name: NISM-Series-V-B: Mutual Fund Foundation Certification
Conducted by: National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM)
Questions: 50 MCQ (No negative marking)
Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
Passing Marks: 25 out of 50 (50%)
Exam Fee: ₹1,200 + GST (approx, verify on nism.ac.in)
Certificate Validity: 3 years (renewal required)
Mode: Online (Computer-based at NISM test centers)
🎯
Eligibility & Process
Who is this for: New cadre of distributors — postal agents, retired govt/bank officials, retired teachers (10+ yrs service)
Scope: Sell only "simple and performing" mutual fund schemes (limited scope vs V-A)
Step 1: Register on nism.ac.in → Create account
Step 2: Book exam slot at nearest NISM center (available PAN India)
Step 3: Appear for exam → Get result immediately
Step 4: PAN provided at registration → Certificate via DigiLocker within 2 weeks
Re-attempt: Can re-appear if failed (check current NISM policy)
💡
V-B vs V-A — Difference
V-B Scope: Simple, performing MF schemes only — limited product range
V-A Scope: Full range of mutual fund schemes — no restrictions
V-B Target: New/entry-level distributor cadre (postal agents, retired officials)
Exam Size: V-B is 50 questions/2hrs vs V-A's 100 questions/2hrs
Want full scope? Consider NISM V-A instead — practice it here
