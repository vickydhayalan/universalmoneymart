NISM Series XV பற்றி அனைத்தும் தெரிஞ்சுக்கோ — Everything You Need to Know
📋
Exam Details
Full Name: NISM-Series-XV: Research Analyst Certification
Conducted by: National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM)
Questions: 80 standalone MCQ + 5 case studies (4 Qs each) = 100 marks
Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
Passing Marks: 60 out of 100 (60%)
Negative Marking: 25% of marks per wrong answer (0.25 deducted)
Certificate Validity: 3 years (renew via CPE or re-exam)
Mode: Online (Computer-based at NISM test centers)
🎯
Why This Certification Matters
Mandatory for: SEBI Research Analyst (RA) registration under SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014
Required to: Publish research reports, issue stock recommendations, or work as a Research Analyst in India
Eligibility: No formal eligibility bar — open to students, professionals, career-switchers
Helpful Background: Commerce/Finance/CA/MBA background makes valuation & accounting units easier
Step 1: Register on nism.ac.in → receive workbook/study material
Step 2: Book exam slot at nearest NISM center
⚠️
Exam Strategy Tips
Case Studies: ~20% of marks are applied case-study questions — not rote recall, practice application-based thinking
Calculative Questions: ~20-25% of the exam involves numerical/calculation-based questions
Negative Marking: Avoid random guessing — a wrong answer costs 0.25 marks
Focus Areas: Company Analysis & Valuation Principles together carry significant weightage
