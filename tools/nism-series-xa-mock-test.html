NISM Series X-A பற்றி அனைத்தும் தெரிஞ்சுக்கோ — Everything You Need to Know
📋
Exam Details
Full Name: NISM-Series-X-A: Investment Adviser (Level 1) Certification
Conducted by: National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM)
Questions: 90 MCQ (1 mark each) + 9 caselets (5 Qs each) = 135 Qs, 150 marks
Duration: 3 Hours (180 Minutes)
Passing Marks: 90 out of 150 (60%)
Negative Marking: 25% of marks per wrong answer
Exam Fee: ~₹3,000 + GST (verify on nism.ac.in)
Certificate Validity: 3 years (renew via CPE or re-exam)
🎯
Why This Certification Matters
Mandatory for: Individual Investment Advisers & Principal Officers under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013
Also for: Associated persons/representatives offering investment advisory services
Level 1 vs Level 2: X-A (Level 1) is foundational; X-B (Level 2) goes deeper for full IA registration
Career Path: Financial planners, wealth advisors, and students entering investment advisory
Step 1: Register on nism.ac.in → receive workbook
Step 2: Book exam slot at nearest NISM center
⚠️
Exam Strategy Tips
Caselets Matter: 60 of the 150 marks come from case-based questions — practice applying concepts, not just recall
Negative Marking: A wrong 1-mark MCQ costs 0.25; a wrong 2-mark caselet question costs 0.5 — avoid careless guessing
Heavy Weightage Areas: Personal Financial Planning and Investment Products together carry substantial marks
Long Exam: 3 hours is long — pace yourself, don't rush the caselets
