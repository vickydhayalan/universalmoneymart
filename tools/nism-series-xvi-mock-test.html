NISM Series XVI பற்றி அனைத்தும் தெரிஞ்சுக்கோ — Everything You Need to Know
📋
Exam Details
Full Name: NISM-Series-XVI: Commodity Derivatives Certification
Conducted by: National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM)
Questions: 100 MCQ, 1 mark each
Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
Passing Marks: 60 out of 100 (60%)
Negative Marking: 25% of marks per wrong answer
Exam Fee: ~₹1,500 + GST (verify on nism.ac.in)
Certificate Validity: 3 years (renew via CPE or re-exam)
🎯
Who Needs This Certification
Mandatory for: Approved users & sales personnel of trading members in the commodity derivatives segment (exchanges like MCX, NCDEX)
Covers: Agricultural commodities (grains, spices, cotton) and non-agri commodities (gold, silver, crude oil, base metals)
Also Useful For: Traders, farmers/processors seeking to hedge price risk, and finance students entering commodity markets
Eligibility: No formal minimum qualification required
Step 1: Register on nism.ac.in → PAN must be updated in profile
⚠️
Exam Strategy Tips
Equal Weightage: Commodity Futures and Commodity Options each carry ~15% — don't skip either segment
Unique to Commodities: Warehousing/physical delivery concepts don't appear in equity/currency derivatives — study these carefully
Negative Marking: Avoid random guessing — a wrong answer costs 0.25 marks
