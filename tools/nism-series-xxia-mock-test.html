NISM Series XXI-A பற்றி அனைத்தும் தெரிஞ்சுக்கோ — Everything You Need to Know
📋
Exam Details
Full Name: NISM-Series-XXI-A: Portfolio Management Services (PMS) Distributors Certification
Conducted by: National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM)
Questions: 100 MCQ, 1 mark each
Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
Passing Marks: 60 out of 100 (60%)
Negative Marking: 10% of marks per wrong answer (lower than most NISM exams)
Certificate Validity: 3 years (renew via CPE or re-exam)
Mandated by: SEBI Notification SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2021/48, dated Sept 7, 2021
🎯
Who Needs This Certification
Mandatory for: Individuals distributing Portfolio Management Services (PMS) on behalf of SEBI-registered Portfolio Managers
Career Roles: PMS Sales Executives, Wealth Managers, Independent Consultants
Note: Distinct from Series XXI-B, which certifies actual Portfolio Managers (not distributors)
Eligibility: No formal minimum qualification, basic financial markets knowledge recommended
Step 1: Register on nism.ac.in → receive workbook
⚠️
Exam Strategy Tips
Lower Negative Marking: Only 10% (vs 25% in most NISM exams) — slightly more forgiving for a calculated guess
Broad Syllabus: Covers stocks, fixed income, derivatives & mutual funds — a strong investment fundamentals base helps
Focus Areas: Discretionary vs Non-Discretionary vs Advisory PMS distinctions are heavily tested
