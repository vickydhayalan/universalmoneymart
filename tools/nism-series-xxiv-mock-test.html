NISM Series XXIV பற்றி அனைத்தும் தெரிஞ்சுக்கோ — Everything You Need to Know
📋
Exam Details
Full Name: NISM-Series-XXIV: AML and CFT Provisions in Securities Markets Certification
Conducted by: National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM)
Questions: 50 MCQ, 1 mark each — the SHORTEST NISM certification exam
Duration: 1 Hour (60 Minutes)
Passing Marks: 25 out of 50 (50%)
Negative Marking: None ✅ — attempt every question confidently
Exam Fee: ~₹885 + payment gateway charges (verify on nism.ac.in)
Certificate Validity: 3 years
🎯
Who Needs This Certification
Launched: A newer NISM certification (2025), reflecting rising AML/CFT compliance focus in India's securities markets
Relevant for: Compliance officers, AML/Principal Officers, and designated staff at brokers, DPs, portfolio managers, investment advisers, research analysts, and MFDs
Also Useful For: Anyone in client onboarding, transaction processing, or risk management roles at SEBI-registered entities
Exam Mode: Remote proctored environment (can be taken from home/office)
Step 1: Register on nism.ac.in → PAN must be updated in profile
💡
Exam Strategy Tips
No Negative Marking: Attempt every question — there's no penalty for a wrong guess
PMLA & Records Rules: Together carry the highest weightage (16 of 50 marks) — study these thoroughly
Case-Based Learning: Real PMLA case discussions appear in the exam — understanding practical application matters, not just definitions
