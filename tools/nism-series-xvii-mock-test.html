NISM Series XVII பற்றி அனைத்தும் தெரிஞ்சுக்கோ — Everything You Need to Know
📋
Exam Details
Full Name: NISM-Series-XVII: Retirement Adviser Certification
Conducted by: National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM)
Questions: 100 MCQ, 1 mark each
Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
Passing Marks: 60 out of 100 (60%)
Negative Marking: 25% of marks per wrong answer
Exam Fee: ~₹1,500 (incl. GST) + payment gateway charges
Certificate Validity: 3 years (renew via CPE or re-exam)
🎯
Who Needs This Certification
Mandatory for: Individuals, proprietors, partners & representatives registered as a Retirement Adviser under PFRDA (Retirement Adviser) Regulations, 2016
Required to: Sell/advise on NPS (National Pension System), or give retirement advice professionally
Regulator: PFRDA (Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority)
Career Fit: Natural extension for MFDs/financial advisers already serving clients nearing retirement
Step 1: Register at certifications.nism.ac.in → receive workbook
⚠️
Exam Strategy Tips
Calculation-Heavy: Real Rate of Return, Annuity Payouts, and Inflation-Adjusted Corpus questions appear frequently — master Time Value of Money math
NPS Structure: Know Tier I vs Tier II accounts, PFM/CRA roles, and annuity purchase requirements at exit cold
Negative Marking: Avoid random guessing — a wrong answer costs 0.25 marks
