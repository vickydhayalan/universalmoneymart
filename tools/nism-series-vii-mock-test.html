NISM Series VII பற்றி அனைத்தும் தெரிஞ்சுக்கோ — Everything You Need to Know
📋
Exam Details
Full Name: NISM-Series-VII: Securities Operations and Risk Management (SORM) Certification
Conducted by: National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM)
Questions: 100 MCQ, 1 mark each
Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
Passing Marks: 50 out of 100 (50%)
Negative Marking: 25% of marks per wrong answer
Exam Fee: ~₹1,500 + GST (verify on nism.ac.in)
Certificate Validity: 3 years (renew via CPE or re-exam)
🎯
Who Needs This Certification
Mandatory for: Associated persons of registered stock-brokers/trading members/clearing members handling client assets, grievances, internal control, or operational risk
Roles Covered: Front, Middle & Back Office staff at broking firms
Also Useful For: Clearing corporations, custodians, and depository participants
Eligibility: No formal minimum qualification prescribed
Step 1: Register on nism.ac.in → receive workbook
Step 2: Book exam slot at nearest NISM center
⚠️
Exam Strategy Tips
Lower Pass Mark: 50% — relatively easier than V-A/VIII/X-A/XV, but the syllabus breadth makes it tricky
Focus Areas: Trade Life Cycle and Risk Management chapters carry heavy weightage
Negative Marking: Avoid random guessing — a wrong answer costs 0.25 marks
