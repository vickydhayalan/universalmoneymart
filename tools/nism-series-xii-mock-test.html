NISM Series XII பற்றி அனைத்தும் தெரிஞ்சுக்கோ — Everything You Need to Know
📋
Exam Details
Full Name: NISM-Series-XII: Securities Markets Foundation Certification
Conducted by: National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM)
Questions: 100 MCQ, 1 mark each
Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
Passing Marks: 60 out of 100 (60%)
Negative Marking: None ✅ — no risk in attempting every question
Exam Fee: ~₹1,000–1,770 + GST (verify current fee on nism.ac.in)
Certificate Validity: 3 years
🎯
Who Needs This Certification
Nature: This is a VOLUNTARY (not mandatory) certification — unlike most other NISM exams
Ideal for: Entry-level professionals starting a career in securities markets, students, and anyone new to investing
Great Starting Point: Covers broad basics before diving into specialised NISM series (like V-A, VIII, X-A)
Eligibility: No minimum qualification required
Step 1: Register on nism.ac.in → receive workbook
💡
Exam Strategy Tips
No Negative Marking: Attempt every single question — there's no penalty for a wrong guess
High Weightage: Securities Types/Asset Allocation, Primary Markets, Secondary Markets, and Mutual Funds each carry ~20% — study these thoroughly
Foundational, Not Advanced: Focuses on breadth of basic concepts rather than deep technical detail
