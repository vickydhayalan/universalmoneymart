June 30, 2026 — The First Spark

11:50 AM, Pinned Chat 💬

Unaku marriage-la real-a interest irukka, illa veetla compel pannuranga nu therinjikkanum nu tha unaku message pannen — pressure pannanum nu illa. Reply panna nee edutha two days-ku ulla veetla commitment pesi, amma-vum "varom" nu solli vittanga. Apparam nee "seri" nu sonna, aana adhu un mulu manasoda sonna mathiri enakku theriyala — "nerla paathutu pesi decide pannalam" nu vittuten. Namba oru chinna purchase-kae 10 thadava yosippom, ithu marriage — kandippa un decision-ku time venum nu enakku theriyum.

Enakku konjam expectations irunthichu — azhaga irukkanum nu, aana rich-ah illa model-ah illa; azhagu life-long irukkathu, understanding thaan life-a nalla run pannum nu nambaren. Adha nenachikkitu iruntha nerathula, curiosity-la un Instagram paathen — confirm aagama irundhaalum, next day same face-la photo share pannirundhanga, apparam "okay sollidalam" nu oru feeling vanthuchu, yenna nu innum enakku theriyala. Adhoda vida, veetla un pathi "hardworking, strong girl" nu sonnadhum enakku innum pudichuchu. Family enna solli irundhaalum, un kitta pesi thaan mudivu edukkanum nu naan ready-ah irunthu message pannen. Ellame sernthu yosichi paakumbothu, oru vishayam mattum clear-a therinjuchu — enakku ellaru mathiri life venaam, naan en partner-ku nalla support-ah irukkanum, avanga support-um enakku venum. Nee un life-a en kooda spend pannura nu nambi, unna unmaiya, nalla paathukkanum nu mattum enakku aasai.
