4. ULIP vs MF — Never ULIP!

❌ ULIP Reality

5-10% charges in Year 1. Net returns only 6-8%. Opaque structure. Avoid for education!
Always: Term Insurance (separate) + Mutual Fund SIP = Much better!

6. Education Loan — Smart Use!

✅ Benefits

80E deduction on interest (8 years, no upper limit!)
Reduces corpus burden. Child builds credit history.
Loan interest 8-10% vs corpus earning 12-14% = net positive!
Use loan to supplement, not replace, the corpus.

7. Common Mistakes

  • ❌ ULIP for education — high charges, low return
  • ❌ FD alone — 6-7% vs 10% inflation = negative real return!
  • ❌ Delay start — 10x more expensive at age 13 vs birth
  • ❌ Touch retirement corpus for education — Use loan instead!
