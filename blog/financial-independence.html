3. FIRE Variants — Which is Yours?

🥗 Lean FIRE

₹30-50K/month · 50%+ savings · Retire age 38-44 · Minimal lifestyle

🏡 Regular FIRE

₹50-100K/month · 40-50% savings · Retire 42-50 · Comfortable life

💎 Fat FIRE

₹1.5L-3L/month · 60-70% savings · Retire 35-45 · Luxury lifestyle

☕ Barista FIRE

Part-time + investments · Semi-retire · Passion work only

🌍 Coast FIRE

Already invested enough. Let compounding do the rest. Work for fun, not money! The most underrated FIRE variant.

4. Savings Rate → Years to FI

⚡ The Secret

Savings rate, not income, determines your FI timeline!
10% → 43 years · 30% → 28 years · 50% → 17 years · 70% → 8 years!
Control lifestyle. Save more. Reach FI faster!

5. FI Portfolio

Accumulation phase (working years): Index 35% + Flexi Cap 30% + Mid Cap 15% + International 10% + Debt 10%.

Distribution phase (post-FI): BAF 40% + Conservative Hybrid 20% + Short Duration 20% + Flexi Cap 15% + Liquid 5%.

6. FI SWP Income — Bucket Strategy

🪣 3 Buckets

Bucket 1: Liquid Fund ₹15-20L — immediate access, 6 months expenses.
Bucket 2: Conservative Hybrid ₹50-60L — monthly SWP source.
Bucket 3: BAF + Flexi Cap ₹2.2Cr+ — long-term growth, refills Bucket 2.

Annual 7% SWP increase for inflation. Corpus outlasts 40 years!

7. FI Traps to Avoid

  • ❌ One More Year Syndrome — Trust the math. When FI number hit → transition!
  • ❌ Lifestyle Inflation — Income grows, lifestyle grows, FI never reached. Fix lifestyle!
  • ⚠️ Sequence of Returns Risk — Bucket strategy protects. Never sell equity in crash years 1-5.
  • ⚠️ Healthcare Under-estimate — Add ₹25-50L healthcare corpus separate. ₹20L+ health cover!
