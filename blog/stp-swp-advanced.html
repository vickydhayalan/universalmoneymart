1. STP — Systematic Transfer Plan

🔄 Definition

Lumpsum amount-ஐ Liquid Fund-ல park பண்ணி, monthly fixed installments-ல Equity Fund-க்கு transfer பண்றது. Cost averaging benefit + Safe entry!

Flow: Lumpsum → Liquid Fund (6-7% earn) → Monthly STP → Equity Fund → Full exposure achieved over 6-12 months.

2. When to Use STP

  • 💰 Annual bonus or windfall received
  • 😰 Market all-time high — fear of lump entry
  • 🏠 Property sale proceeds (₹20L+)
  • 💼 EPF/EPS withdrawal large amount
  • 🔄 Debt corpus → equity (rebalancing/goal switch)

5. SWP — Systematic Withdrawal Plan

💸 Definition

Mutual Fund corpus-ல இருந்து monthly fixed amount automatically redeem → Bank account credit.
Remaining units continue growing. Only GAIN portion taxed. Principal = Zero Tax!

6. 4% Safe Withdrawal Rule

📊 The Rule

Annual 4% withdrawal → corpus 30+ years sustainable. Corpus grows alongside SWP!

8. SWP Tax — Super Efficient!

🧾 Example

₹1 Crore invested → ₹1.5 Crore value. Monthly ₹40,000 SWP.
Principal portion: ₹26,667 → Zero Tax!
Gain portion: ₹13,333 → LTCG 12.5% = ₹1,667 tax only.
Net income: ₹38,333. Far better than FD!

9. STP + SWP — Complete Lifecycle

🔄 Accumulation (STP)

  • Lumpsum received
  • Park in Liquid Fund
  • Monthly STP → Equity
  • 6-12 months complete
  • Full equity exposure!

💸 Distribution (SWP)

  • Goal reached / Retirement
  • Corpus in Balanced Hybrid
  • Monthly SWP setup
  • 4% annual rule
  • Income for 30+ years!

10. Bucket Strategy for Retirement

BucketTimelineFundPurpose

♾️ The Flow

Equity (Bucket 3) → tops up Debt (Bucket 2) → feeds SWP (Bucket 1). Never run out of income!

11. Common SWP Mistakes

  • 🚫 Withdrawal > 4% — Corpus depletes fast
  • 🚫 100% equity in SWP — Crash = sell at low
  • 🚫 No inflation-adjust — Purchasing power drops
  • 🚫 No bucket strategy — Emergency disrupts income
✅ Right Approach

4% rule · Balanced Hybrid · Annual 7% SWP increase · 3-bucket strategy · Review annually!
