4. Optimal Strategy — Triple Layer

🏆 Best Approach

Layer 1: EPF + VPF — Guaranteed 8.25%, tax-free. Safety base.
Layer 2: NPS ₹50,000/year — Extra tax benefit if 30% bracket.
Layer 3: Mutual Fund SIP — Main wealth builder. Index + Flexi Cap + Mid Cap.

All three together = Complete retirement ecosystem!

5. Retirement SWP Blueprint

💸 4% Rule

₹3 Crore corpus → 4% annual withdrawal = ₹1,00,000/month.
Annual 7% increase for inflation. Corpus grows alongside SWP.
Balanced Hybrid Fund + Bucket Strategy = 30+ year income!

6. Inflation in Retirement — Hidden Risk!

₹1L/month today needs ₹1.97L in 10 years, ₹3.87L in 20 years at 7% inflation!

💡 Solution

Annual SWP 7% increase mandate. Keep 20-30% equity even in retirement for growth. Corpus must grow alongside withdrawals!
