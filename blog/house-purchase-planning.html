1. 3 Golden Rules of Home Buying

20%

Down payment rule. Never borrow for DP!

<40%

Total EMI of take-home income. Sacred!

Age 60

Retire before home loan ends. Non-negotiable!

2. Rent vs Buy — Real Decision

✅ Buy When

Same city 7+ years confirmed · 20% DP ready · EMI < 40% · EF intact · Retirement SIP running · No other major loans

❌ Rent When (Or Wait)

Job uncertain / transfers possible · DP not ready (don't rush!) · EMI would exceed 40% · Young 20s → equity MF returns > real estate · Rent < 3% of property value → Rent wins!

💡 Key Rule

NEVER borrow for down payment. Save in Balanced Advantage Fund (BAF) for 5-7 years. Let compounding build your DP!

4. Home Loan Interest — The Shocking Reality

⚠️ Real Numbers

₹50 Lakh loan @ 8.5%, 20 years:
Monthly EMI: ₹43,400 · Total paid: ₹1.04 Crore! · Interest alone: ₹54 Lakhs!
You pay 2x the property value over 20 years.

✅ Smart Move

Same ₹50L loan, 10-year tenure: Total ₹74.3L, interest only ₹24.3L! Shorter tenure + annual pre-payment = Save ₹30L+!

6. Tax Benefits — ₹3.5L/Year!

🧾 Double Benefit

Section 80C: Principal repayment → ₹1.5L deduction
Section 24B: Interest payment → ₹2L deduction
Total: ₹3.5L per year tax deduction!
Section 80EEA (affordable housing): ₹1.5L additional if eligible.

7. Right Time to Buy

Personal factors > market timing. When all 6 checks pass:

  • ✅ Same city 7+ years confirmed
  • ✅ 20% DP ready from own savings
  • ✅ EMI < 35-40% of take-home
  • ✅ Emergency fund 6+ months intact
  • ✅ Retirement + education SIPs running
  • ✅ No other major loans running

8. Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Max loan eligibility use — EMI > 40%, goals suffer
  • ❌ Personal loan for DP — Double EMI from day 1!
  • ⚠️ Floating rate ignore — Budget for 1-2% rate increase
  • ⚠️ No pre-payment — Pay ₹54L interest on ₹50L loan needlessly!
